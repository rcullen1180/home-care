Questions

How often do you visit?

As often as you like. Most people choose once a day or every other day, and overnight stays are available for longer trips.

What happens if something goes wrong?

We call you right away, send photos, and can meet a plumber, electrician, or other pro at the house so you don't have to come home.

Is my booking confirmed when I send the form?

Not yet. The form sends a request, and we'll reach out within a day to confirm and set up a walk-through.

How do you handle keys and alarm codes?

We collect them at the walk-through, keep them private, and return them or leave them where you ask when you're back.
